Data Deletion Instructions

How to request permanent deletion of the personal data PlayBeat Digital holds about you, including data received through Meta (Facebook & Instagram).

Option 1 — Delete via Facebook or Instagram (recommended for Meta data)

1.Open Settings & Privacy → Settings in your Facebook or Instagram app.
2.Go to Apps and Websites (Facebook) or Website permissions → Apps and websites (Instagram).
3.Select MyPlaybeat / PlayBeat Digital and tap Remove.
4.Meta notifies us automatically through our Data Deletion Request Callback, and we erase the associated data. You receive a confirmation code on this page when the request is processed.

Deletion requests received through Meta are processed within 30 days.

Option 2 — Request deletion directly

Email privacy@playbeat.digital with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • The email address or phone number on your PlayBeat account
  • Optionally, your recent order number(s) to speed up matching

We confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete deletion within 7 days, with a written confirmation to your email.

What gets deleted — and what we must keep

Deleted: your account profile, contact messages, support chats, marketing preferences, reviews, and device/analytics identifiers tied to you.

Retained (as required by law): order and invoice records are retained in anonymized form where tax and consumer-protection rules require it — they can no longer be linked back to you personally.

Your deletion confirmation code

Keep this code for your records — it identifies your deletion request.
